Delete your bento/id account

bento/id is run by __CONTROLLER__ · Contact: __CONTACT_EMAIL__

You can delete your bento/id account from any web browser. You don't need bento/home or any other app, and you never type a code from an email.

With one of your passkeys

  1. Open __DELETE_URL__ and sign in with one of the account's passkeys.
  2. Press “Delete this account”, read what it does, and confirm with your passkey.
  3. We email your address to say the deletion is waiting. The email names the passkey that asked and lists all of them.

Planned If you no longer have any of your passkeys

    Planned
  1. Open the same page and enter your email address.
  2. We email that address a link. Open it, and press the button on the page it opens.

Until this is running, ask for a reset on the same page instead. It gives the account one new passkey after 72 hours, and that passkey can then delete it.

What happens next

  • The deletion takes effect 72 hours after you ask. Every change to a bento/id account waits this long, so that someone who has your passkey, or your mailbox, can't delete your account before you notice.
  • The email that says it is waiting carries a link that stops it. If you didn't ask, press Cancel, and nothing is deleted. The passkey that asked then can't ask for anything for 72 hours. If you don't recognise that passkey, the same link can suspend it instead, and it is removed 72 hours later.
  • A reset asked for while the deletion waits cancels the deletion for good: stopping the reset doesn't bring it back.
  • Once it takes effect, your passkeys stop working for this account, and people can no longer check new work against it. Work signed before then shows “account deleted”. __DELETION_RETENTION__
  • You can set up a new account for the same address at any time. It starts new: people who knew the old one see that it changed.
  • Your documents are files you keep. Deleting the account doesn't touch them.

Help

If you can't use either way, write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__ from the account's address. The privacy notice lists everything bento/id keeps, and for how long.
