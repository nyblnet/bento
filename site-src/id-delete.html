Delete your bento/id account

You can delete your bento/id account from any web browser. You don't need bento/home or any other app, and you never type a code from an email.

With one of your passkeys

  1. Open __DELETE_URL__.
  2. Enter your email address, and confirm with one of the account's passkeys.
  3. We email your address to say the deletion is waiting.

If you no longer have any of your passkeys

  1. Open the same page and enter your email address.
  2. We email that address a link. Open it, and press the button on the page it opens.

What happens next

  • The deletion takes effect 72 hours after you ask. Every change to a bento/id account waits this long, so that someone who has your passkey, or your mailbox, can't delete your account before you notice.
  • The email that says it is waiting carries a link that stops it. If you didn't ask, press it, and nothing is deleted.
  • Once it takes effect, your passkeys stop working for this account, and people can no longer check new work against it. __DELETION_RETENTION__
  • Your documents are files you keep. Deleting the account doesn't touch them.

Help

If you can't use either way, write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__ from the account's address. The privacy notice lists everything bento/id keeps, and for how long.
