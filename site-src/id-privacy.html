bento/id privacy notice

Effective __EFFECTIVE_DATE__ · Controller: __CONTROLLER__ · Contact: __CONTACT_EMAIL__

bento/id lets people find your Bento key from your email address, so they can share documents with you and see that a collaborator is really you. It is run by a small directory service at id.bento.page. This notice covers that service. Bento documents themselves are files you keep; we never host them in bento/id.

What the directory stores

What it never stores

  • Your documents, or anything inside them.
  • Your private keys.
  • Who you share with, or a list of your contacts.
  • Who looked up your address. Lookups are not logged against you.

Verification codes and email

To publish or remove an address we email it a one-time code. The code expires after 20 minutes and is stored only as a hash. Email is sent through Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES), in the European Union, which acts as our processor. Like any email sender, it sees the address and the time a message is sent. Messages contain only the code and fixed wording, with no tracking.

Network information

To stop abuse we limit how often one network can ask for codes or lookups. For this we keep a one-way, daily-changing fingerprint of your IP address, and a counter, for at most a day. We do not store IP addresses themselves.
